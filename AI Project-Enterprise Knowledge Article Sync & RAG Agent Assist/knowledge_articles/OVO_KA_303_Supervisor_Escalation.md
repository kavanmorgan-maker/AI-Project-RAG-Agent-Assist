# KB Article 303: Complaint Handling & Supervisor Escalation Matrix

## Overview
Guidelines for frontline agents managing dissatisfied customers, hostile interactions, or formal Ombudsman complaints.

## De-escalation & Agent Authorization
Agents are empowered to resolve issues at first contact using the following tools:
- **Goodwill Gesture Authorization:** Up to £30 credit balance adjustment for service delays without supervisor sign-off.
- **Payment Arrangements:** Extend payment due dates by up to 14 days or pause collections activities for 30 days during active disputes.

## Mandatory Supervisor Escalation Triggers
An agent MUST escalate a call to a Tier-2 Team Leader if:
- The customer requests a formal Deadlock Letter to take to the Energy Ombudsman.
- The complaint involves legal action, regulatory breach threats (Ofgem), or media involvement.
- The complaint has reached **Day 45** of an unresolved dispute (approaching the mandatory 8-week limit).
- The caller displays abusive or threatening behavior after 2 polite warnings (follow Refusal of Service protocol).
