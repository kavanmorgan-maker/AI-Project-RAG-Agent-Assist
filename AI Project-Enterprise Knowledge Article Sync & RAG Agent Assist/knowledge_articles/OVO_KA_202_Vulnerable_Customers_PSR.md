# KB Article 202: Priority Services Register (PSR) & Emergency Guidelines

## Overview
The Priority Services Register (PSR) is a free service providing extra support to vulnerable energy customers during supply outages or financial hardship.

## Eligibility Criteria
A customer qualifies for PSR if they meet any of the following:
- Rely on electricity for home medical equipment (e.g., dialysis, oxygen concentrators, stairlifts).
- Are over state pension age, disabled, or have a chronic long-term health condition.
- Have young children (under 5 years old) living at the property.
- Require communications in accessible formats (Braille, Large Print, Audio).

## Agent Action Protocol for Active Outages
1. **Identify High Risk:** Ask if medical equipment depends on active power.
2. **Immediate Flagging:** If YES, flag the ticket as **CRITICAL_MEDICAL_OUTAGE** in the CRM.
3. **Dispatch:** Trigger the Emergency Dispatch protocol for priority backup heating/generators via the Network Operator (DNO).
4. **Registration:** If not already registered, log customer details into the PSR database with customer consent.
