# KB Article 101: Data Protection Act (DPA) & ID Verification Procedure

## Overview
Before disclosing any account information or making changes to an OVO account, the agent must successfully verify the caller's identity.

## Standard Identification Verification (3-Point Check)
To pass standard DPA, the customer must confirm:
1. Account Holder's Full Name
2. Full Service Address (including Postcode)
3. Date of Birth OR Last 4 digits of the bank account linked to Direct Debit

## Special Scenarios
- **Third-Party Callers:** Information can only be discussed if an "Authority to Act" (ATA) flag or Power of Attorney is logged in the CRM system.
- **Failed DPA:** If the caller fails two verification attempts:
  1. Do not reveal any financial balances or personal details.
  2. Offer to send a One-Time Passcode (OTP) to the registered mobile number or email on file.
  3. If OTP verification fails, direct the caller to upload ID via the online portal and end the call securely.
